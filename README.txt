AI SURVEILLANCE - KAISE CHALAYE (Windows)

1. setup.bat pe double-click (sirf ek baar)
2. run_webcam.bat  -> webcam pe chalega ('q' = band)
3. Video ke liye: video.mp4 naam se is folder me rakho, run_video.bat chalao
4. Zone chunna: pick_zone_webcam.bat ya pick_zone_video.bat, 4 corners click, Enter
5. Dashboard: run_dashboard.bat (browser me khulega, Ctrl+C = band)

Settings (LOCATION, thresholds, weights) surveillance.py ke upar CONFIG me hain.
